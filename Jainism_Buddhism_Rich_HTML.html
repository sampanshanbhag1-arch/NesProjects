Causes for the Rise

Why They Emerged

People were dissatisfied with complex rituals, costly sacrifices and social inequalities. Merchants and urban populations wanted simpler teachings. Both religions emphasized ethical conduct, personal effort, equality and spiritual progress, making them attractive to common people.

Jainism

Introduction

Jainism is one of the oldest religions of India. It was propagated by the 24th Tirthankara, Mahavira. The religion teaches self-discipline, non‑violence, truthfulness and liberation from the cycle of birth and death through right conduct.

Jainism is one of the oldest religions of India. It was propagated by the 24th Tirthankara, Mahavira. The religion teaches self-discipline, non‑violence, truthfulness and liberation from the cycle of birth and death through right conduct.

Main Teachings of Jainism

Core Ideas

The Three Jewels are Right Faith, Right Knowledge and Right Conduct. The Five Great Vows are Ahimsa, Satya, Asteya, Brahmacharya and Aparigraha. Jainism stresses strict self-control, karma, rebirth and respect for every living being.

Impact of Jainism

Contributions

Jainism promoted non‑violence and vegetarianism. It influenced literature, philosophy, architecture and trade communities. Beautiful temples at Dilwara and Ranakpur reflect its artistic achievements. Jain values continue to influence Indian society.

Decline of Jainism

Reasons

Strict rules were difficult for ordinary people. Loss of royal support in some regions reduced expansion. Competition from Hinduism and Buddhism, along with limited missionary activities, contributed to its decline in many areas.

Buddhism

Introduction

Buddhism was founded by Gautama Buddha. After attaining enlightenment at Bodh Gaya, he taught a path to end suffering. His teachings emphasized compassion, wisdom, morality and meditation.

Buddhism was founded by Gautama Buddha. After attaining enlightenment at Bodh Gaya, he taught a path to end suffering. His teachings emphasized compassion, wisdom, morality and meditation.

Teachings of Buddhism

Core Ideas

The Four Noble Truths explain suffering and its causes. The Eightfold Path includes right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration. Buddhism promotes the Middle Path and compassion toward all.

The Four Noble Truths explain suffering and its causes. The Eightfold Path includes right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration. Buddhism promotes the Middle Path and compassion toward all.

Impact of Buddhism

Contributions

Buddhism spread throughout Asia and influenced art, architecture, education and international cultural exchange. Stupas, monasteries and universities such as Nalanda became important centers of learning and culture.

Conclusion

Comparison

Both Jainism and Buddhism opposed ritualism and promoted ethical living. Both believed in karma and rebirth. Jainism emphasized strict non‑violence while Buddhism advocated the Middle Path. Their influence remains visible in modern society.
